# Reference Brands for Vibe Marketing

These are real AI brands that turned a clear vibe into powerful marketing. Learn from their tone, positioning, and content style.

---

## 1. Creatify.ai

### Brand Vibe
Fast, bold, performance-driven. "We make ads that sell, not just look pretty."

### Brand Kit
- Name: Creatify
- Tagline: "AI ads that convert"
- Colors: Electric blue (#3B82F6), black (#000000), white (#FFFFFF), neon green accent (#22C55E)
- Typography: Modern sans-serif, bold headers, clean body text
- Voice: Confident, data-driven, no-nonsense

### What They Do
- Paste a product URL → AI generates video ads automatically
- 1500+ lifelike AI avatars, 29 languages
- A/B test hundreds of ad variations in minutes
- Platforms: Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Snap, Amazon

### Marketing Style
- Hook-first: lead with the result, not the feature
- UGC-style video ads (looks organic, not corporate)
- Heavy use of before/after comparisons
- Social proof: "40+ G2 badges", "4.8/5 rating", "$24M raised"
- Customers: Alibaba, Zumper, NewsBreak

### Ad Copy Examples

**Awareness hook:**
"You're still spending $5K per video ad? We make 100 for $50."

**Consideration:**
"Creatify users cut ad production costs by 70%. Same quality. 10x faster."

**Conversion:**
"Paste your product link. Get a video ad in 2 minutes. Try free."

### Landing Page Pattern
- Hero: big bold claim + product demo GIF + single CTA
- Social proof bar: logos of known customers
- 3 feature cards with short copy
- Pricing section with free tier highlighted
- Footer: minimal, trust badges

## 2. HeyGen

### Brand Vibe
Professional, trustworthy, human-like. "Your digital twin, but better."

### Brand Kit
- Name: HeyGen
- Tagline: "Create videos as easily as writing an email"
- Colors: Deep purple (#7C3AED), white (#FFFFFF), soft gray (#F3F4F6), coral accent (#F97316)
- Typography: Rounded sans-serif, friendly but professional
- Voice: Warm, approachable, empowering

### What They Do
- Type a script → AI avatar delivers it on video
- Avatar IV: hand gestures, facial expressions, natural body language
- Translate videos into 175+ languages with lip-sync
- Custom brand avatars for enterprise

### Marketing Style
- Demo-first: show the avatar talking, then explain
- Side-by-side: real person vs AI avatar (the "wow" moment)
- Focus on time saved: "From script to video in 5 minutes"
- Enterprise trust signals: "100,000+ businesses", "G2 #1 Fastest Growing"
- Emotional angle: "Scale yourself without burning out"

### Ad Copy Examples

**Awareness hook:**
"This person doesn't exist. But they just closed a $10M deal for our client."

**Consideration:**
"100,000 businesses use HeyGen to create videos 10x faster. Zero camera, zero crew."

**Conversion:**
"Create your first AI video free. No credit card. 2 minutes."

### Landing Page Pattern
- Hero: AI avatar video playing on loop + "Create free video" CTA
- "How it works" 3-step section (type, choose avatar, generate)
- Use case tabs: marketing, sales, training, support
- Customer logos + testimonial carousel
- Pricing with annual discount highlighted

## 3. Manus (by Meta)

### Brand Vibe
Intelligent, autonomous, enterprise-grade. "The agent that does your job while you sleep."

### Brand Kit
- Name: Manus
- Tagline: "Your AI agent for advertising"
- Colors: Meta blue (#1877F2), dark navy (#1E3A5F), white (#FFFFFF), signal green accent (#00C853)
- Typography: Clean system font (similar to Meta's design system), minimal
- Voice: Authoritative, calm, technical but accessible

### What They Do
- Autonomous AI agent inside Meta Ads Manager
- Builds reports, researches audiences, analyzes campaigns automatically
- Operates across campaigns (not just optimizing one)
- Available to 4M+ advertisers

### Marketing Style
- Authority positioning: "Acquired by Meta for $2B"
- Workflow before/after: manual process vs agent doing it
- Scale emphasis: "4 million advertisers", "across all campaigns"
- Technical credibility + simple explanation
- No hype — let the product speak

### Ad Copy Examples

**Awareness hook:**
"What if your ads manager worked 24/7 and never made a mistake?"

**Consideration:**
"Manus analyzes your campaigns, finds patterns, and recommends changes — while you focus on strategy."

**Conversion:**
"Open Ads Manager. Click Tools. Manus is already there. Start now."

### Landing Page Pattern
- Hero: clean, minimal, one sentence + screenshot of Manus in Ads Manager
- "What Manus does" — 3 cards: Report, Research, Optimize
- Integration diagram showing Manus inside the Meta ecosystem
- "Already available" — no waitlist, just use it
- Enterprise footer with Meta branding

## Patterns to Copy

### What all 3 brands do well:

| Pattern | Creatify | HeyGen | Manus |
|---------|----------|--------|-------|
| Hook style | Result-first ("100 ads for $50") | Demo-first (show avatar) | Problem-first ("what if") |
| Social proof | G2 badges, funding, logos | User count, G2 award | Meta acquisition, scale |
| CTA | "Try free" | "Create free video" | "Already in your tools" |
| Tone | Bold, fast | Warm, empowering | Calm, authoritative |
| Key metric | Cost savings (70%) | Speed (10x faster) | Scale (4M advertisers) |
| Video style | UGC / performance ads | Avatar demos | Product screenshots |

### Hook Formulas That Work:

1. **Shock number:** "You're spending $X. We do it for $Y."
2. **Impossible claim:** "This person doesn't exist. But they just..."
3. **What-if:** "What if your [tool] worked 24/7?"
4. **Speed flex:** "From [input] to [output] in [time]."
5. **Social proof lead:** "[Number] businesses already use this to..."

### Landing Page Blueprint:

1. Hero: bold claim + visual proof + single CTA
2. Social proof bar: logos or numbers
3. "How it works": 3 steps max
4. Use cases or features: 3-4 cards
5. Testimonial or case study
6. Pricing (if applicable)
7. Final CTA repeat
8. Minimal footer
