# Reference Brands for Clothing Advice

Four fashion tiers — from bohemian accessible to haute couture. Learn each brand's styling rules to give advice that matches the user's aesthetic.

---

## 1. Anthropologie

### Vibe
Bohemian romantic. Whimsical but not childish. "The woman who travels, reads, and decorates beautifully."

### Style DNA
- Textured fabrics: linen, crochet, embroidered cotton, lace
- Earthy + jewel tones: terracotta, sage, dusty rose, mustard, ivory
- Flowy silhouettes: maxi dresses, wide-leg pants, draped blouses
- Layered accessories: stacked rings, woven bags, statement earrings
- Prints: florals, paisley, patchwork, watercolor

### Spring 2026 Key Pieces
- Barrel-leg denim (Kayden jeans)
- Petticoat lace pants
- Asymmetric sleeveless lace slip dresses
- Satin wide-leg trousers
- Kitten heels with squared toes

### Styling Rules
- Mix textures: pair lace top with denim, knit cardigan over silk dress
- One statement piece per outfit, keep rest neutral
- Accessories should feel collected, not matched (vintage feel)
- Footwear: mules, block heels, woven sandals — never sporty
- Hair and makeup: effortless, natural, soft waves

### When User Scans This Vibe, Suggest:
- "This has an Anthropologie feel — romantic and textured"
- Pair recommendations: "Try a linen blazer over that dress with woven sandals"
- Color advice: "Swap the black for sage green to soften the look"

## 2. Zara

### Vibe
Minimalist cool. Runway looks made affordable. "Looks expensive, costs less."

### Style DNA
- Clean lines, structured silhouettes
- Neutral palette: black, white, camel, navy, gray, olive
- Modern tailoring: oversized blazers, wide-leg trousers, sharp coats
- Minimal accessories: thin gold chain, simple leather bag, clean sneakers
- No logos, no loud prints — let the cut speak

### Spring 2026 Key Pieces
- Wide-leg exaggerated trousers
- High funnel-neck outerwear
- Slim minimal sneakers (no chunky logos)
- Slouchy striped knits
- Soft-touch suede and faux fur textures

### Styling Rules
- Monochrome or tonal dressing (head-to-toe camel, all-black, cream layers)
- One oversized piece balanced by one fitted piece (big blazer + slim pants)
- Accessories: less is more — one clean bag, one simple necklace
- Footwear: pointed flats, minimal sneakers, leather boots — nothing fussy
- Hair: slicked back, low bun, or straight — controlled and sharp

### When User Scans This Vibe, Suggest:
- "This is very Zara — clean, minimal, modern"
- Pair recommendations: "Add an oversized blazer and pointed flats to elevate this instantly"
- Color advice: "Stick to one color family — go tonal for maximum impact"

## 3. Chanel

### Vibe
Timeless Parisian elegance. Quiet luxury with edge. "Unapologetically feminine, never trying too hard."

### Style DNA
- Tweed: jackets, skirts, coats — the signature fabric
- Two-tone: black/white, navy/cream, pink/black
- Structured but soft: tailored jackets with rounded shoulders
- Chain details: bags, belts, jewelry — gold chain is signature
- Pearls: layered, oversized, mixed with casual pieces

### 2026 Direction (Matthieu Blazy)
- Squished brand codes: frayed tweed, blanket-texture suits
- Ultrasoft two-tone flats (praline-inspired)
- Layered interpretation: high fashion meets morning commute
- Precision tailoring with quiet knits
- Animal prints used sparingly as accent

### Styling Rules
- The Chanel jacket: pair with jeans for casual, skirt for formal — it works both ways
- Always one chain element (bag strap, belt, necklace)
- Pearls: layer 2-3 different lengths, mix with casual outfit for contrast
- Two-tone shoes elongate the leg
- Never overdone — if the jacket is statement, keep everything else simple
- Makeup: red lip OR smokey eye, never both

### When User Scans This Vibe, Suggest:
- "This has Chanel energy — elegant with a bit of edge"
- Pair recommendations: "Add a structured tweed jacket and pearl earrings to make this effortlessly chic"
- Color advice: "Black and cream is your power combo here"

## 4. Dior

### Vibe
Romantic glamour. Architectural femininity. "Fashion as art — every piece tells a story."

### Style DNA
- The Bar jacket: nipped waist, full skirt — the Dior silhouette
- Sculptural shapes: peplum, lampshade skirts, cage structures
- Luxe fabrics: Chantilly lace, metallic jacquard, silk organza, shearling
- Soft muted tones: almond, blush, powder blue, champagne
- Bold accessories: saddle bag, statement belts, oversized sunglasses

### 2026 Direction (Jonathan Anderson)
- Lightness over structure: spiral cage dresses now in soft pleated fabric
- 18th-century aristocratic codes reimagined: frock coats, bustle skirts
- Baby-soft shearling blazers
- Dotted Swiss ruffle skirts with long trains
- Sculptural knits — cozy but architectural
- Narrative dressing: each outfit tells a character story

### Styling Rules
- Cinch the waist — the Dior rule. Belt everything.
- One architectural piece per outfit (the rest should be simple)
- Muted palette with one accent (blush outfit + red lip, cream + gold bag)
- Skirt length matters: midi for elegance, above-knee for modern
- Footwear: pointed pumps, kitten heels, slingbacks — always polished
- Hair: done, not undone. Chignon, soft curls, side part.

### When User Scans This Vibe, Suggest:
- "This is giving Dior — structured, romantic, glamorous"
- Pair recommendations: "Cinch that waist with a statement belt and add a pointed heel"
- Color advice: "Soft almond and blush tones would elevate this beautifully"

## Color Pairing Cheat Sheet

| If wearing... | Pair with... | Avoid... |
|--------------|-------------|----------|
| All black | Gold accessories or red lip | Silver (too cold) |
| White / cream | Tan, camel, soft gold | Bright colors (too harsh) |
| Navy | White, cream, cognac | Black (too close, looks muddy) |
| Blush / pink | Gray, cream, soft gold | Orange (clashes) |
| Earth tones | Ivory, denim, olive | Neon (breaks the mood) |
| Red | Black, nude, gold | Pink (unless intentional) |
